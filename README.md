# Vista Ride

Ride your exercise bike through sunny real-world routes: Alps & Dolomites, Italian coast & lakes, France, Macedonia, Croatian coast and Alpine lakes.

- Pick a region (or one route) and a ride length from 30 minutes to 2 hours.
- Connect a Bluetooth bike (FTMS, Cycling Power or Speed & Cadence, e.g. Yesoul A1) and the video follows your pedalling.
- Add your own routes by pasting a YouTube link.

Open in Chrome on Android and use **Add to Home screen**. Add `?demo` to the address to try it with simulated pedalling.

Videos stream from the YouTube channels that filmed them.
